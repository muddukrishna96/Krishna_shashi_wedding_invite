Travel & stay

Nearest railway station is Umaria, followed by Katni and then Jabalpur. The nearest airport is Jabalpur.

If you're flying into Delhi, the train is the better option from there — it's about 13 hours from Delhi to Umaria directly.

Rooms are available at Hotel Royal Ananda for outstation guests — do let us know your travel dates and we'll help with the booking.

If you're planning to arrive on the 28th or 29th, do send us a note so we can plan pick-up.
